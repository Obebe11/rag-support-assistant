# Returns Reminder for the Holiday Season

Notice. Version 1.0. Effective November 1, 2026.

This notice reminds customers how returns work during the busy end-of-year period. The Returns Policy continues to apply without changes.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. Every return needs an RMA number. Request it on the Orders page of the account portal or by email at returns@northline.example.com.

## Opened items

An opened item in like-new condition, with all accessories, manuals and packaging, is refunded at the item price less a 15% restocking fee.

## Processing times

RMA requests are answered within 1 business day.
