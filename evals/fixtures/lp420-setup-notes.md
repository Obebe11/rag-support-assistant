# LP-420 Setup Notes

Product note. Version 1.0. Effective October 1, 2026.

These notes help you get the Northline LP-420 Thermal Label Printer ready for first use.

## Connecting the printer

Connect the LP-420 to a computer with the USB cable from the box, or to your office network by Ethernet. The LP-420 does not have Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

## Drivers

Download the printer driver from the Downloads section of the account portal. Drivers are available for Windows 10, Windows 11, and macOS 12 or later.

## Loading labels

Open the cover, place the roll on the holder with the labels facing up, and feed the first label under the guides. Close the cover. The printer calibrates the label gap automatically.

## First test print

Hold the feed button for 3 seconds to print a test label.
