# Warranty Policy

Version 4.0. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Scope

Northline Office Equipment warrants the equipment it sells against defects in materials and workmanship under normal office use. The warranty applies to the original purchaser and is not transferable.

## Warranty periods

Every warranty period starts on the delivery date.

| Product | Warranty period |
| --- | --- |
| P-240 Monochrome Laser Printer | 2 years |
| C-360 Color Multifunction Printer | 2 years |
| LP-420 Thermal Label Printer | 2 years on the printer; 6 months on the print head |
| S-300 Cross-Cut Shredder | 2 years on the machine; 5 years on the cutting head |
| L-125 Thermal Laminator | 1 year |
| Optional paper trays | 1 year |
| Toner cartridges | 90 days |

Laminating pouches, labels, shredder oil and waste bags are consumables. They are not covered by this warranty.

## Proof of purchase

Your Northline invoice number or order number is your proof of purchase. You do not need to register the product. We also ask for the serial number of the unit, which is printed on the label on the back or the bottom of the product.

## What the warranty covers

- Defects in materials and workmanship that appear during the warranty period under normal use within the product's published specifications.
- Parts and labor for covered repairs.
- Shipping in both directions for covered repairs. We provide prepaid shipping labels.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental damage, such as drops, impacts and liquid spills.
- Damage caused by misuse, such as shredding materials that the S-300 is not rated for or laminating without a pouch.
- Damage caused by power surges, fire, flood or other external events.
- Use beyond the product's published monthly duty cycle.
- Wear parts that have reached the end of their rated life, such as printer fusers and rollers.
- Repairs or modifications made by anyone other than Northline.
- Units with a removed or altered serial number.
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect how the product works.

## Third-party supplies

Using third-party toner, labels or laminating pouches does not by itself void the warranty. Damage or failures caused by third-party supplies are not covered. Repairs for that kind of damage are billed at the out-of-warranty rates in the Service and Maintenance Guide.

## Remedies

For a covered defect, we will, at our option, repair the unit, replace it with a new or refurbished unit of equal or better specification, or refund the purchase price if repair or replacement is not possible.

A repaired or replacement unit is covered for the rest of the original warranty period or for 90 days, whichever is longer.

## How to make a warranty claim

1. Contact the Technical Service Desk at (800) 555-0164 or service@northline.example.com. Have the serial number and your invoice or order number ready.
2. A technician works with you by phone or email to find the cause. Many problems are resolved at this step.
3. If the unit has a hardware fault, we open a service case and email a prepaid shipping label.
4. Ship the unit to our service center within 10 business days of receiving the label.
5. We repair or replace the unit and ship it back. Our standard turnaround is 5 business days from the day the unit arrives at the service center.

## Advance replacement

Advance replacement is available for the P-240 and the LP-420 during the first 12 months of the warranty period. We ship a replacement unit on the next business day, before you send back the defective unit.

- You need a credit card or a Net 30 credit account on file.
- Ship the defective unit back with the prepaid label within 10 business days of receiving the replacement. If we do not receive it, we charge the list price of the replacement unit.
- Advance replacement is not available for the C-360, the S-300 or the L-125. These products are repaired at the service center.

## Northline Care extended coverage

- Northline Care extends the warranty period by 1 or 2 years on the same terms.
- It is available for the P-240, the C-360, the LP-420 and the S-300. It is not available for the L-125 or for consumables.
- Buy it with the equipment or within 60 days of the delivery date.
- The price is 10% of the product's list price for 1 additional year, or 18% of the list price for 2 additional years.
- Northline Care does not extend the 6-month coverage of the LP-420 print head or the 5-year coverage of the S-300 cutting head.

## Limit of liability

Northline's liability under this warranty is limited to the purchase price of the product.
