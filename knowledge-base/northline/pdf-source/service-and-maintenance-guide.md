# Service and Maintenance Guide

Version 1.4. Effective May 1, 2026.

This guide explains the service options for Northline equipment, the cost of repairs outside the warranty, and the routine maintenance each product needs.

## Service options

| Option | When it applies | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remote troubleshooting by phone or email | Any Northline product, in or out of warranty | Free |
| Warranty repair at the service center | A covered defect during the warranty period | Free, including shipping both ways |
| Advance replacement | P-240 and LP-420 during the first 12 months of the warranty period | Free |
| Out-of-warranty repair at the service center | After the warranty ends, or for damage the warranty does not cover | Diagnostic fee plus parts and labor |

## Contacting the Technical Service Desk

- Phone: (800) 555-0164
- Email: service@northline.example.com
- Hours: Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific Time, and Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Pacific Time (phone only)

Have this information ready: the model, the serial number, your order or invoice number, a description of the problem, and any error code on the display.

## Out-of-warranty repair

- The diagnostic fee is $65.00 per unit. If you approve the repair, the fee is credited toward the repair cost.
- We send a written estimate within 2 business days after the unit arrives at the service center.
- Labor is billed at $95.00 per hour. Parts are billed at list price.
- If you decline the estimate, we return the unit at your expense. The diagnostic fee is not refunded.
- You pay for shipping to the service center. We pay for Standard Ground shipping back to you after a completed repair.
- An approved repair is usually completed within 7 business days of approval.
- Out-of-warranty repairs are covered for 90 days against the same fault.

## Printer maintenance

### P-240 Monochrome Laser Printer

| Task | When |
| --- | --- |
| Replace the toner cartridge | When the display shows "Replace toner" |
| Clean the paper feed rollers with a damp lint-free cloth | Every 6 months, or sooner if paper misfeeds |
| Replace the maintenance kit NL-MK240 (fuser and rollers), $149.00 | Every 100,000 pages |
| Update the firmware | When the account portal lists a new version |

You can replace the P-240 maintenance kit yourself. It takes about 15 minutes, and instructions are included with the kit.

### C-360 Color Multifunction Printer

| Task | When |
| --- | --- |
| Replace a toner cartridge | When the display shows which color to replace |
| Clean the scanner glass and the document feeder glass strip | Every week, or when copies show streaks |
| Replace the waste toner container NL-WT360, $24.00 | Every 30,000 pages |
| Replace the maintenance kit NL-MK360 (fuser, transfer roller and feed rollers), $289.00 | Every 150,000 pages |

Clean the scanner glass with a lint-free cloth. Put glass cleaner on the cloth, not directly on the glass.

### LP-420 Thermal Label Printer

| Task | When |
| --- | --- |
| Clean the print head with a cleaning pen (NL-CP5) or an isopropyl alcohol swab | Every time you load a new roll of labels |
| Clean the platen roller | Every month |
| Replace the print head NL-PH420, $59.00 | When labels show vertical white lines that cleaning does not remove |

Turn the LP-420 off and wait 2 minutes before you clean the print head.

## Shredder maintenance

### S-300 Cross-Cut Shredder

- Oil the cutting head after every 30 minutes of total shredding time or every time you empty the bin, whichever comes first.
- Use Northline shredder oil (NL-OIL12). Apply the oil across a sheet of paper in a zigzag pattern, shred the sheet, and then run the shredder in reverse for 10 seconds.
- Do not use aerosol lubricants or petroleum-based oils. They are a fire hazard and can damage the cutting head. That damage is not covered by the warranty.
- Empty the bin when the bin-full indicator lights up. Do not press shredded paper down to make more room.
- If the shredder stops and the overheat indicator is on, it has reached its 20-minute continuous run limit. Let it cool down for 40 minutes.
- To clear a jam, hold the reverse button for 3 to 5 seconds, remove the paper, and feed fewer sheets. The limit is 18 sheets per pass.
- Unplug the shredder before you clean the feed opening or the sensors.

## Laminator maintenance

### L-125 Thermal Laminator

- Clean the rollers with a cleaning sheet (NL-CS125) once a month, or right away if you see adhesive on the rollers or marks on finished pouches. Run the cleaning sheet through twice while the laminator is warm.
- Always feed the sealed edge of the pouch first. Never laminate a document without a pouch.
- To clear a jam, pull the jam release lever and remove the pouch by hand.
- Let the laminator cool down for 30 minutes before you store or move it.
- Do not use oil, solvents or sharp tools on the rollers.

## Spare parts and supplies

- Northline keeps spare parts for each model for at least 5 years after the model is discontinued.
- Order parts and supplies in the account portal or through Customer Support. Have the model and serial number ready.

## Firmware and manuals

- Firmware and user manuals are in the Downloads section of the account portal. Downloads are free.
- The P-240 and the C-360 check for new firmware once a month when they are connected to the internet.
- LP-420 firmware is updated over USB with the file from the Downloads section.

## Preparing a unit for service

- Pack the unit in its original packaging if you still have it. If not, ask the Technical Service Desk for a packing kit. The kit is free for warranty repairs and costs $12.00 for out-of-warranty repairs.
- Remove the toner cartridges from a P-240 or a C-360 before shipping and keep them. Toner can leak in transit.
- Remove paper, labels and pouches. Empty the bin of an S-300.
- Export the address book of a C-360 before shipping. The service center resets every C-360 to factory settings.
- Write the service case number on the outside of the box.

## Service case status

Each repair has a service case number. To check the status of a repair, contact the Technical Service Desk with the case number.
