# Northline C-360 Color Multifunction Printer

Product specification sheet. Version 1.1. Effective March 1, 2026.

SKU: NL-C360. List price: $1,249.00.

## Overview

The C-360 is a color laser multifunction printer for workgroups of 5 to 15 people. It prints, copies, scans and faxes, and it is operated from a 7-inch color touchscreen.
