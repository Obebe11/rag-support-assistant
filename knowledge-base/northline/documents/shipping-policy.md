# Shipping Policy

Version 3.0. Effective June 1, 2026.

This policy explains how Northline Office Equipment ships orders, what shipping costs, and what to do when a shipment arrives damaged, incomplete or late. It covers deliveries within the United States.

## Delivery area

- Orders to the 48 contiguous states can use any shipping method in this policy.
- Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship by Two-Day Air only. The rate is based on the carrier's charges for the package and is shown before you confirm the order.
- We ship to street addresses only. We cannot ship to PO boxes.

## Order processing

- In-stock orders placed by 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time on a business day ship the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time, on a weekend or on a company holiday ship the next business day.
- Next-Day Air has an earlier cut-off. A Next-Day Air order must be placed by 12:00 noon Pacific Time to ship the same day.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, except the company holidays listed in the Contact and Support Hours document.

## Parcel shipping methods and rates

Parcel rates apply to orders with a total shipping weight of 150 lb or less that are delivered within the 48 contiguous states.

| Method | Delivery time after shipment | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Ground | 3 to 5 business days | $14.95 per order; free on orders of $500 or more |
| Two-Day Air | 2 business days | $39.00 per order |
| Next-Day Air | 1 business day | $79.00 per order |

- Standard Ground shipping is free when the merchandise subtotal, after any discounts, is $500 or more. Free shipping does not apply to Two-Day Air, Next-Day Air, or orders to Alaska and Hawaii.
- The Two-Day Air and Next-Day Air flat rates apply to orders with a total shipping weight of 70 lb or less. Heavier air shipments are quoted before you confirm the order.
- Delivery times are carrier estimates. They are not guaranteed delivery dates.

## Freight shipping

- Within the 48 contiguous states, orders with a total shipping weight over 150 lb ship on a pallet by less-than-truckload (LTL) freight. Delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after shipment.
- Standard LTL freight is free when the merchandise subtotal, after any discounts, is $2,500 or more. Below $2,500, we quote the freight charge before you confirm the order.
- Standard freight delivery is curbside: the driver brings the pallet to the curb or to a loading dock.
- Liftgate service costs $45.00 per delivery. You need it if the delivery address has no loading dock or forklift.
- Inside delivery costs $95.00 per delivery. The driver moves the pallet just inside a ground-floor entrance.
- Liftgate and inside delivery charges apply even when the freight itself is free.
- The freight carrier calls the delivery contact on the order to schedule a delivery appointment.

## Tracking

We email a tracking number on the day the order ships. Tracking is also shown on the Orders page of the account portal. When an order ships in more than one box, each box has its own tracking number. We do not charge extra shipping for split shipments.

## Signature on delivery

Orders with a merchandise subtotal of $1,000 or more require a signature at delivery.

## Address changes and refused deliveries

- Before an order ships, Customer Support can change the delivery address at no charge.
- After an order ships, the carrier charges $18.00 per package for an address correction. A correction can add 1 to 2 business days to delivery.
- If you refuse delivery of an undamaged order, we treat it as a return. The refund is reduced by the return shipping cost and a 15% restocking fee, even if the package is unopened.

## Damaged, missing or lost shipments

- Inspect the shipment when it arrives. If the packaging is visibly damaged, note the damage on the carrier's delivery receipt before you sign.
- Report damaged, missing or incorrect items to Customer Support within 5 business days of delivery. Include the order number and photos of the item and its packaging. Keep the packaging until the claim is resolved.
- For problems reported within 5 business days of delivery, we ship a replacement or issue a refund at no cost to you. We also pay for return shipping.
- If a parcel has not arrived within 7 business days after the ship date, contact Customer Support. We open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement shipment at no charge.
