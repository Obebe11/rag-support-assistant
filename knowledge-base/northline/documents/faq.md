# Frequently Asked Questions

Version 2.3. Effective September 1, 2026.

Short answers to common questions about buying from Northline Office Equipment. The policy documents have the full terms.

## Accounts and ordering

### Do I need an account to place an order?

Online orders need an account. Creating one is free and asks for your business name, a contact person and a delivery address. If you order by phone or email without an account, we create one for you and email the login details.

### Do you sell to individuals?

Yes. Our products and terms are designed for businesses, but individuals and home offices can order at the same prices.

### How many people can use one account?

One account can have up to 10 users, each with a separate login. The account owner adds users on the Users page of the account portal and chooses who can place orders and who can only view them.

### Is there a minimum order?

No. Orders with a merchandise subtotal under $50 carry a $7.50 small-order fee.

### How do I know whether an item is in stock?

Each product page in the account portal shows one of three statuses: In stock, Low stock or Backordered. If you need more than 25 units of one item, ask Sales to confirm availability.

### How do I get a copy of an invoice?

Open the Billing page of the account portal, or ask Billing at billing@northline.example.com.

## Shipping and delivery

### How much does shipping cost?

Standard Ground shipping costs $14.95 per order and is free on orders of $500 or more delivered within the 48 contiguous states. The Shipping Policy lists the rates for faster methods and for freight.

### How do I track my order?

We email a tracking number on the day the order ships. You can also open the Orders page of the account portal.

### Do you deliver on weekends?

No. Our carriers deliver Monday through Friday. An order that ships on a Friday does not move over the weekend.

## Returns

### How long do I have to return an item?

You have 30 days from the delivery date. Every return needs an RMA number. The Returns Policy explains the conditions and the restocking fee for opened items.

### Can I return toner that I have opened?

No. Consumables cannot be returned once the package has been opened. A toner cartridge that is defective is covered by the Warranty Policy for 90 days from delivery.

## Warranty and service

### How long is the warranty?

The printers and the shredder have a 2-year warranty, and the L-125 laminator has a 1-year warranty. Two parts have their own periods: 6 months for the LP-420 print head and 5 years for the S-300 cutting head. The Warranty Policy lists the coverage for each product.

### Do I have to register my product?

No. Your Northline invoice or order number is your proof of purchase.

### Where can I find manuals, drivers and firmware?

They are in the Downloads section of the account portal. Downloads are free. Printer drivers are available for Windows and macOS.

## Products and supplies

### Which toner fits my printer?

The P-240 uses NL-T240 (standard) or NL-T240X (high-yield) toner. The C-360 uses the NL-T360 series: NL-T360K, NL-T360C, NL-T360M and NL-T360Y. P-240 and C-360 cartridges are not interchangeable.

### Can I use other brands of laminating pouches in the L-125?

Yes. The L-125 accepts any thermal laminating pouch from 3 mil to 10 mil thick and up to 12.5 inches wide.

### What labels does the LP-420 use?

The LP-420 uses direct thermal labels from 1 inch to 4.25 inches wide, on rolls with a 1-inch core or as fanfold stacks. It does not use ink, toner or ribbon.

### Can I try a product before placing a large order?

Yes. If you are considering 10 or more units of one model, you can borrow an evaluation unit for 14 days. The Volume Orders and Quotes document has the details.
