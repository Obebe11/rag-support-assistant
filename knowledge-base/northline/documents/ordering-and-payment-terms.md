# Ordering and Payment Terms

Version 2.1. Effective April 1, 2026.

These terms explain how to place an order with Northline Office Equipment, which payment methods we accept, and how to change or cancel an order.

## How to place an order

- Online: use the account portal at northline.example.com/account at any time.
- Email: send the SKUs, quantities and delivery address to orders@northline.example.com.
- Phone: call Sales at (800) 555-0117 during sales hours.
- Purchase order: customers with an approved Net 30 credit account can email a purchase order to orders@northline.example.com.

We email an order confirmation within 2 business hours of receiving the order.

## Prices and minimum order

- All prices are per unit, in US dollars, and do not include shipping.
- The price on your order confirmation is final for that order. List prices for future orders can change without notice. A written quote holds its prices for 30 days.
- There is no minimum order. Orders with a merchandise subtotal under $50 carry a $7.50 small-order fee.

## Payment methods

| Method | When the order ships |
| --- | --- |
| Credit or debit card | Right away. The card is charged when the order ships. |
| ACH bank transfer | After we receive the payment, usually 1 to 2 business days. |
| Wire transfer | After we receive the payment. Accepted for orders of $5,000 or more. |
| Company check | After the check clears, up to 5 business days after we receive it. |
| Net 30 terms | Right away, for customers with an approved credit account. |

We do not accept cash or cash on delivery (COD).

## Net 30 credit accounts

- To apply, complete the credit application from the account portal or request it from billing@northline.example.com. The application asks for your legal business name, two trade references and one bank reference.
- We review credit applications within 3 business days.
- Until the account is approved, pay by card, ACH transfer, wire transfer or check.
- The starting credit limit is $5,000. You can request an increase after 6 months of on-time payments.
- Invoices are due 30 days from the invoice date. The invoice date is the ship date.
- Early payment discount: if you pay an invoice within 10 days of the invoice date, you can deduct 2% of the merchandise total.
- Late payment: overdue balances carry a finance charge of 1.5% per month.
- Credit hold: when an account is more than 15 days past due, new orders do not ship until the overdue balance is paid.

## Order changes and cancellations

- You can change or cancel an order at no charge at any time before it ships. Contact Customer Support with your order number.
- An order that has shipped cannot be canceled. The Returns Policy applies instead.
- Special-order items are non-stock items and custom configurations that we order at your request. They are marked "Special order" on the quote and on the order confirmation. You can cancel a special-order item at no charge within 24 hours of the order confirmation. After 24 hours, it cannot be canceled or returned.

## Backorders

- If an item is out of stock, we email you within 1 business day with an estimated ship date.
- You can wait for the item, switch to a different item, or cancel the backordered item at no charge.
- In-stock items on the same order ship right away. We do not charge extra shipping when the backordered item ships later.
- Cards are charged only for the items that have shipped.

## Invoices

- We email the invoice to the billing contact on the day the order ships.
- Copies of all invoices are available on the Billing page of the account portal.
- For billing questions, contact Billing at billing@northline.example.com or (800) 555-0191.
