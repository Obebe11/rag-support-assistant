# Volume Orders and Quotes

Version 1.2. Effective April 1, 2026.

This document explains volume discounts, how to request a quote, and the terms that apply to larger orders from Northline Office Equipment.

## Volume discounts

Volume discounts are applied automatically. They are based on the merchandise subtotal of a single order at list prices.

| Merchandise subtotal at list prices | Discount on the whole order |
| --- | --- |
| $2,500.00 to $4,999.99 | 5% |
| $5,000.00 to $14,999.99 | 8% |
| $15,000.00 or more | 12% |

- The discount applies to equipment and supplies on the order. No discount code is needed.
- Volume discounts cannot be combined with other promotional offers. The 2% early payment discount for Net 30 accounts still applies.
- For orders over $50,000, contact Sales for project pricing.

## Requesting a quote

- Request a quote by email at quotes@northline.example.com or on the Quotes page of the account portal.
- Include the SKUs and quantities, the delivery ZIP code, the date you need delivery, and any special requirements such as liftgate service.
- We send standard quotes within 1 business day. Quotes that include freight shipping or special-order items can take up to 2 business days.
- A quote is valid for 30 days from the quote date. Prices and discounts on the quote are held for that period.
- To accept a quote, reply with a purchase order number if you have a Net 30 credit account, or approve the quote in the account portal and pay.

## Deposits

For orders of $10,000 or more from customers without an approved Net 30 credit account, we require a 30% deposit when the order is placed. The balance is due before the order ships.

## Lead times

- Quantities of up to 25 units of one item usually ship from stock under the standard order processing times.
- For more than 25 units of one item, allow up to 10 business days before shipment. The quote states the expected ship date.

## Scheduled deliveries

A volume order can be split into up to 3 scheduled deliveries within 90 days of the order date, at the quoted price. Each delivery is invoiced when it ships.

## Shipping for volume orders

The Shipping Policy applies to volume orders. Standard Ground shipping is free on orders of $500 or more, and standard LTL freight is free on orders of $2,500 or more. Both thresholds are based on the merchandise subtotal after discounts. Liftgate service and inside delivery are charged separately.

## Evaluation units

A business that is considering 10 or more units of one model can borrow one evaluation unit for 14 days. Northline pays shipping in both directions. An evaluation unit that is not returned within 14 days is invoiced at list price. Request an evaluation unit from Sales.

## Account managers

Customers who spend $25,000 or more with Northline in a calendar year are assigned a named account manager. All other customers work with the Sales team.
