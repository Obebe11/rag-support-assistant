# Contact and Support Hours

Version 1.5. Effective August 1, 2026.

This document lists the teams at Northline Office Equipment, how to reach them, and when they are available. All times are Pacific Time.

## Customer Support

Customer Support handles orders, shipping, delivery problems and account questions.

- Phone: (800) 555-0142
- Email: support@northline.example.com
- Hours: Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

## Sales and quotes

Sales handles product questions, volume orders, quotes and evaluation units.

- Phone: (800) 555-0117
- Email: sales@northline.example.com
- Quote requests: quotes@northline.example.com
- Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

## Technical Service Desk

The Technical Service Desk handles troubleshooting, warranty claims and repairs.

- Phone: (800) 555-0164
- Email: service@northline.example.com
- Hours: Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
- Saturday service is by phone only. Emails received on Saturday are answered on the next business day.

## Returns

- Email: returns@northline.example.com
- RMA requests are answered within 1 business day.

## Billing

Billing handles invoices, payments and Net 30 credit accounts.

- Phone: (800) 555-0191
- Email: billing@northline.example.com
- Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

## Response times

- We answer emails within 1 business day.
- Voicemail left outside business hours is returned on the next business day.

## Website chat

The chat assistant on the Northline website is available at all hours. When the assistant passes a conversation to a person, the team replies during Customer Support hours. Requests received outside those hours are answered on the next business day.

## Escalations

If your issue is not resolved within 2 business days, ask for a support supervisor or write to escalations@northline.example.com. A supervisor replies within 1 business day.

## Company holidays

Northline is closed on these days. Orders placed on a holiday ship on the next business day.

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and the day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Day
