# Northline P-240 Monochrome Laser Printer

Product sheet. Version 1.2. Effective March 1, 2026.

SKU: NL-P240. List price: $389.00.

## Overview

The P-240 is a black-and-white laser printer for small offices and workgroups of up to 10 people. It prints on both sides of the page automatically and connects by USB, Ethernet or Wi-Fi.
