# Returns Policy

Version 2.0. Effective September 1, 2026. This version replaces version 1.3.

This policy explains when and how you can return items bought from Northline Office Equipment, and how refunds are calculated.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The delivery date is the date shown in the carrier's tracking record.

## Return authorization

- Every return needs a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number.
- Request an RMA number on the Orders page of the account portal or by email at returns@northline.example.com. Include the order number, the item and the reason for the return.
- We issue RMA numbers within 1 business day.
- An RMA number is valid for 14 days from the date we issue it. Ship the return within that period.
- Write the RMA number on the outside of the box. We refuse packages that arrive without a valid RMA number and send them back at the sender's expense.

## Item condition and refund amount

| Condition of the returned item | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Unopened, in the original sealed packaging | 100% of the item price |
| Opened, in like-new condition with all accessories, manuals and packaging | Item price less a 15% restocking fee |
| Defective, damaged in transit or shipped in error | 100% of the item price plus the original shipping charge |
| Used, damaged by the customer or missing parts | Not eligible for return |

- A laser printer or multifunction printer counts as like-new only if its page counter shows 100 pages or fewer. A printer that has printed more than 100 pages is not eligible for a change-of-mind return.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is caused by a defect, transit damage or our error.

## Return shipping

- For a change-of-mind return, you pay the return shipping. Use a service with tracking.
- For defective items, items damaged in transit and items shipped in error, we email a prepaid return label.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Consumables such as toner, laminating pouches, labels and shredder oil, once the package has been opened.
- Special-order items, as defined in the Ordering and Payment Terms.
- Items sold as clearance or final sale.
- Items returned after the 30-day return window.

## Refunds

- We issue the refund to the original payment method within 7 business days after the return arrives at our returns center.
- For orders paid on a Net 30 credit account, we apply a credit memo to the account instead.
- We send an email confirmation when the refund is issued.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange an item, return it under this policy and place a new order for the item you want.

## Equipment that is defective on arrival

If equipment does not work when you first use it, report it to the Technical Service Desk within 30 days of delivery. We replace it with a new unit and email a prepaid return label for the defective one. After 30 days, defects are handled under the Warranty Policy.
