# Shipping Policy

Version 2.2. Effective January 15, 2025. Superseded by version 3.0 on June 1, 2026.

This policy explains how Northline Office Equipment ships orders and what shipping costs. It covers deliveries within the United States.

## Delivery area

- Orders to the 48 contiguous states can use any shipping method in this policy.
- Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship by Two-Day Air only. The rate is shown before you confirm the order.
- We ship to street addresses only. We cannot ship to PO boxes.

## Order processing

- In-stock orders placed by 12:00 noon Pacific Time on a business day ship the same day.
- Orders placed after 12:00 noon Pacific Time, on a weekend or on a company holiday ship the next business day.

## Parcel shipping methods and rates

Parcel rates apply to orders with a total shipping weight of 150 lb or less that are delivered within the 48 contiguous states.

| Method | Delivery time after shipment | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Ground | 4 to 6 business days | $12.95 per order; free on orders of $750 or more |
| Two-Day Air | 2 business days | $35.00 per order |
| Next-Day Air | 1 business day | $72.00 per order |

Delivery times are carrier estimates. They are not guaranteed delivery dates.

## Freight shipping

- Orders with a total shipping weight over 150 lb ship on a pallet by less-than-truckload (LTL) freight. Delivery takes 5 to 8 business days after shipment.
- We quote the freight charge before you confirm the order.
- Liftgate service costs $40.00 per delivery.

## Tracking

We email a tracking number on the day the order ships.

## Damaged, missing or lost shipments

- Inspect the shipment when it arrives. If the packaging is visibly damaged, note the damage on the carrier's delivery receipt before you sign.
- Report damaged, missing or incorrect items to Customer Support within 3 business days of delivery. Include the order number and photos of the item and its packaging.
